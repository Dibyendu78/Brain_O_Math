About Brain-O-Math Olympiad

Organized by {{ event_config.organizer_name }} , the Brain-O-Math Olympiad {{ event_config.event_year }} is an interschool competitive exam in Mathematics and Science that evaluates students' logical reasoning, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. The exam provides a platform for young learners to showcase their academic talent, build confidence, and prepare for higher-level challenges.

Our Mission

To develop interest in Mathematics and Science among young minds through innovation, excellence, and healthy competition that prepares students for future challenges.

Excellence Standards

High-quality question papers, fair evaluation process, and prestigious recognition that motivates students to achieve their best potential.

Recognition & Rewards

Solid Silver Medal, certificates of excellence, and opportunities for higher-level competitions to celebrate achievement.

School Partnership

Collaborative approach with schools to identify and nurture mathematical and scientific talent through structured competitive examinations.

EXAM PATTERN

Mode: Offline OMR-based exam
Questions: 50 MCQs per subject
Total Marks: 60 per subject
Duration: 1 hour per subject
Marking: No negative marking

SYLLABUS

  • The examination is based on the concepts and learning outcomes generally covered in the respective school curriculum and is suitable for students studying under CBSE, ICSE, and State Boards.
  • The question paper for each category is designed with an appropriate level of difficulty so that students from both classes within the category can attempt it fairly and confidently. The examination emphasizes conceptual clarity, logical reasoning, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
  • The detailed syllabus is available on our website.
