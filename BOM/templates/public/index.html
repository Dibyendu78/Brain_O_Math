About the Olympiad

Structured competition for school learners

Organized by Doon Heritage School, Siliguri, the Brain-O-Math Olympiad 2025 is an interschool assessment in Mathematics and Science. It supports critical thinking, reasoning, and confidence-building through a fair and well-managed competitive process.

The program is designed to create a positive experience for students, coordinators, and schools. It follows transparent procedures for question design, evaluation, and result publication.

Focused learning

Assessment resources and exam structure motivate strong analytical skills across classes 3 to 12.

Fair evaluation

Clear marking guidelines and timely result updates support credibility for students and teachers.

School collaboration

Coordinators manage student registration and communication directly, making participation easy for schools.

Exam details

Registration, pattern and schedule

Registration and fees

Online registration is open for coordinators. Schools may also use the handbook for offline registration and payment.

  • Single subject fee: ₹70
  • Both subjects fee: ₹140
  • Payment methods accepted: UPI and cash

Exam pattern

The examination uses OMR sheets and multiple-choice questions to ensure quick evaluation and fair scoring.

  • 50 MCQs per subject
  • Total marks: 60 per subject
  • Duration: 1 hour per subject
  • No negative marking

Exam schedule

Classes 3 to 12 follow a structured timeline with a short break between subjects.

  • Mathematics: 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM
  • Break: 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM
  • Science: 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM

Result and award delivery

Results are shared with coordinating teachers and award certificates are distributed through schools.

  • Result declaration: 16 December 2025
  • Certificates and awards delivered by 18 December 2025
  • Communication via Email and WhatsApp
